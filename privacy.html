Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wallpix Gallery, developed by DevDrey Studios, handles information when you use the application. We aim to keep your information secure and use it only as necessary to provide and improve the service.
Section 01

Information We Collect

Wallpix Gallery may collect limited information necessary to provide account functionality and operate the application.

Depending on how you use the application, this may include:

  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Display name associated with your account.
  • Authentication information required to sign you in.
  • Information necessary to maintain your application account.

If you use Google Sign-In, authentication may be handled through Google's authentication services.

Section 02

How We Use Information

Information collected by Wallpix Gallery may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Authenticate you when you sign in.
  • Provide application features and services.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect the application against misuse or abuse.
Section 03

Authentication and Firebase

Wallpix Gallery uses Firebase Authentication to support account registration and sign-in.

Depending on the available sign-in options, users may authenticate using email and password or Google Sign-In.

Account-related information may be stored and processed using Google Firebase and Google Cloud services.

Section 04

Cloud Storage and Wallpaper Delivery

Wallpaper images and related application content may be stored, cached, or delivered through third-party infrastructure used by Wallpix Gallery.

These services are used to provide reliable and efficient access to wallpaper content.

Section 05

Advertising

Wallpix Gallery displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

Depending on the application experience, advertisements may include native advertisements, app open advertisements, and rewarded video advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and interactions with advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable settings.

Important: Wallpix Gallery does not sell your personal information directly to third parties.

Section 06

Notifications

If notification functionality is enabled, Wallpix Gallery may use notification services to send application-related messages, updates, or other relevant information.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings where supported.

Section 07

Children and General Audience

Wallpix Gallery is designed as a general-audience application and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of internet-connected applications and services.

Advertising and other third-party services used by the application may have their own age-related requirements and privacy controls.

Section 08

Third-Party Services

Wallpix Gallery relies on certain third-party services to provide application functionality.

These may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Google Cloud services.
  • Google AdMob.
  • Google Sign-In.
  • Other infrastructure providers required to deliver application content.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Section 09

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Wallpix Gallery accounts and services.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Section 10

Data Retention

Account information may be retained for as long as necessary to maintain your account and provide the application service.

Information may also be retained where necessary for security, legal, technical, or operational purposes.

If you would like your account deleted, you can submit a request through our account deletion form.

Section 11

Privacy Choices

You may control certain permissions through your device settings, including notification permissions and other applicable application permissions.

Advertising preferences may also be controlled through Google's available advertising and privacy settings.

Section 12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Wallpix Gallery, our services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Section 13

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Wallpix Gallery handles information, you can contact DevDrey Studios.
